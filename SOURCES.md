## Editorial benchmark

- Save My Exams was reviewed for generic revision-design principles: clear topic hierarchy, concise explanations, examiner cautions, purposeful diagrams and a direct route to practice. No protected notes, questions, diagrams, branding or page design were copied.

## Past papers

- Direct PDF base used for established past-paper links:  
  https://pastpapers.papacambridge.com/directories/CAIE/CAIE-pastpapers/upload
- Filename pattern: `[syllabus]_[series][year]_[qp|ms]_[component].pdf`
- PapaCambridge subject and session pages were inventoried for every available session from 2020 through 23 October 2026.
- Verified inventory total: **4,693 component rows** and **9,312 live direct question-paper/mark-scheme PDFs** across 33 established syllabus codes.
- Every direct URL was checked on 23 October 2026 using an HTTP HEAD request requiring a PDF response without redirection. Fourteen stale PapaCambridge listings were excluded.
- The bank includes March, May/June and October/November sessions whenever PapaCambridge lists them, plus all live listed paper variants.
- IGCSE Psychology 0266 has no genuine past-paper series before first assessment in 2027, so the site labels and links official Cambridge specimen material instead of inventing PapaCambridge filenames.
